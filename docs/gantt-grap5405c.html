Project timeline (Gantt chart) builder

AT1B — 2D Design Process. The phases, tasks and milestones are set out across the 16-week semester. You decide how long each task takes, what depends on what, and when each milestone must be finished.

How to use this & why it matters

Design work follows a predictable order of operations. This chart already lays out the phases, the tasks and the milestones for the deck project. Your job is to turn it into a real plan: decide how long each task takes, what has to happen before what, and when each phase must be signed off.

What to do

  1. Fill in your name, student ID and anyone you planned with.
  2. For each task, click a week on the timeline to place its bar, then drag the bar to move it and drag its ends to make it longer or shorter. Click an empty week to jump the bar there, or double-click a bar to clear it and start again. The length of the bar is how long you think the task will take.
  3. Add an hours estimate for each task. The summary tells you if you're under or over typical times.
  4. At the top, drag each coloured ◆ to the week that milestone is due or signed off (the assessment tasks, plus your concept sign-off). A matching coloured line runs down the chart so you can see which tasks fall before each gate — use it to check your workload is realistic.
  5. Add or delete tasks (✕) if your project needs it, then export to Word and insert it into your AT1B worksheet.

You can plan this with others — list who in the "Planned with" field. Your plan should still reflect your own project. It's assessed, but there's no single right answer: what's checked is that you understand the sequence, the dependencies and realistic effort.
