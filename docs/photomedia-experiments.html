      What to write in your AT3d report

      Do two experiments. For each one, save a screenshot. Then write three sentences.

      1. I tried ...

      2. I hoped it would ...

      3. It worked / did not work because ...

      An experiment that did not work still counts. Say what you learned.

      Can I use AI?

      Yes: any generative AI tool to help finish your composite. This includes Photoshop's built-in tools (Generative Fill, Generative Expand, Harmonize, Lens Blur) and other tools like Nano Banana in Gemini, if you have access.

      No: replacing everything with AI. Your AT3 must still be a composite of photos. Use AI to help join, light or finish the photos, not to make the picture for you.

      AI gives you a guess, not an answer. You decide if it worked. Zoom to 100% and check:

      Does the light come from the same side as the rest?

      Does the texture and grain match the photo around it?

      Are there smudges, odd shapes or extra objects?

      Can you see where the AI part starts and stops?

      If it fails, that is a good experiment to write about. Say what went wrong.

      Before you start

      Save a copy of your file first. Then you can always go back.

      Use adjustment layers where you can. They do not change your pixels.

      Toggle the eye icon on and off to compare before and after.
